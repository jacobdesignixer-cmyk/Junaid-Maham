# Luxury Wedding Invitation — Junaid & Maham

An elegant, standalone wedding invitation website replica customized for **Junaid Jehangir & Maham Jamal** in **Karachi, Pakistan**.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Short Opening Preloader**: Luxury gold "Wedding Invitation" emblem and shimmer line (1.1s).
- **Silk Reveal Hero Video**: Interactive tap to begin with animated silk flowing reveal.
- **Romantic Background Music**: Loops continuously throughout the invitation.
- **Floating Gold Music Button**: Animated vinyl-disc control button to pause/resume sound anytime.
- **Save the Date Card**: **Friday, 25 December 2026 at 07:00 PM** in **Karachi, Pakistan**.
- **Live Countdown Timer**: Real-time Days, Hours, Minutes, and Seconds until December 25, 2026.
- **Full Wedding Itinerary**: Timeline of events (Arrival, Nikah Ceremony, Dinner, Celebration).
- **Venue & Google Maps**: Venue showcase with direct map navigation link.
- **Dress Code & RSVP**: Elegant formal attire swatches and attendance confirmation.
